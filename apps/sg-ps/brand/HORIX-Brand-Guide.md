# HORIX Visual Identity

The official vector master is `master/horix-master.svg`. All derived assets in this package are generated from its original paths without redrawing or altering the symbol geometry.

## Brand Position

HORIX is the visionary AI image editor by SABER GROUP. The identity combines the forward vision of Horus with the speed and transformation represented by `X`.

Primary descriptor: `THE VISIONARY AI EDITOR`

## Logo System

- Use `horix-symbol-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.
- Use `horix-symbol-light.svg` on light backgrounds.
- Use the monochrome symbols only when color reproduction is unavailable.
- Use `horix-wordmark-dark.svg` and `horix-wordmark-light.svg` for the complete official logo lockup on splash screens and marketing surfaces.
- Use `horix-app-icon.svg` for application launchers and PWA assets.
- Use `horix-favicon.svg` at browser-tab sizes.

The amber eye is the intelligence signal. It must remain a small accent and should not become a dominant brand color.

## Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horus Ink | `#141314` | Primary background and light-mode logo |
| Vision Amber | `#EA993F` | Eye, intelligence and active-state signal |
| Horix Red | `#D63C32` | The `X`, primary actions and critical emphasis |
| Falcon Ivory | `#F3EFE8` | Dark-mode logo and primary text |
| Graphite | `#2A2727` | Panels, dividers and secondary surfaces |

## Clear Space

Keep clear space around the complete logo equal to the height of the `X` stroke. Do not place controls, text, or container edges inside this area.

## Minimum Sizes

- Complete logo lockup: `96px` wide minimum.
- Falcon symbol: `24px` minimum.
- Favicon: use the dedicated simplified asset below `24px`.

## Typography

- Display and headlines: a condensed geometric grotesk with heavy weight.
- Descriptor, product UI and body copy: Inter, using Regular, Medium, and Bold.
- The complete lockup uses `THE VISIONARY AI EDITOR`, a short Horix Red rule, then `BY SABER GROUP`.
- Keep letter spacing at `0` in the product interface.

## Usage Rules

- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add shadows to the logo.
- Do not replace Vision Amber or Horix Red with blue or purple.
- Do not add pyramids, hieroglyphs, or ornamental Egyptian motifs.
- Do not use gradients inside the core mark.
- Do not place the dark logo on low-contrast backgrounds.

## Voice

Precise, confident, intelligent, and forward-looking. HORIX helps creators see further; it does not present itself as a novelty AI effect.
